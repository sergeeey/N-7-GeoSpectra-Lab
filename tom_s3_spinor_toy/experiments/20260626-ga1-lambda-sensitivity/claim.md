# GA1 Claim — Lambda Sensitivity of m_mod/m_KK

**Question type:** descriptive

**Claim:** The ratio m_mod/m_KK computed in G91 varies by less than a factor of 3
across the physically reasonable range lambda in [0.15, 0.60].

**Natural language statement:**
We estimate the spread of m_mod/m_KK for the S³×S⁶ compactification
comparing lambda=0.15 vs lambda=0.60, using the same G91 canonical-radion
formalism, to determine whether Bottleneck 1 (free lambda) is critical
for the order-of-magnitude physics.

**What this does NOT mean:**
1. Does NOT fix lambda — it remains a free parameter (Bottleneck 1 still open)
2. Does NOT validate any specific lambda value as physically preferred
3. Does NOT apply outside the range [0.15, 0.60] without separate check
