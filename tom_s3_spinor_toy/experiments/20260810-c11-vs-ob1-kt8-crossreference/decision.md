## Why S3 fails on closer inspection

C41–C57's entire arc investigates one question: **does forming a spectral triple out of both
`t=0` and `t=1` together (as two sectors of one Hilbert space) satisfy NCG's own axioms** —
algebra (C45/C46), real structure (C48, W1/Y1/Y2 → C50–C54), orientability/Poincaré duality
(C49, C52), and the KO tuple (C55–C57).

**The answer, across the whole chain, is no** — the doubling is not earned, and even taken
as given, the resulting even triple fails orientability and Poincaré duality (C49, C52).

That is a **derivation map about the *combination*** — a genuinely new one, and the strongest
methodological finding of the day. But OB1 does not ask about the combination. OB1 asks
whether **one** `t` (single copy, no doubling at all) is selected over Levi-Civita for KT-8's
product Dirac operator on `S³×S⁶`. **Those are different objects** (table in `claim.md`), and
a verdict about one does not transfer to the other — the same non-transfer discipline this
project's `artifact-provenance-gates.md` Gate 1 requires between any two related-but-distinct
artifacts, applied here to two related-but-distinct *questions*.

If anything, C41–C57's result works in the **opposite** direction from what a reopening would
need: it **rules out** one candidate answer to "why would nature care about `t=0` and `t=1`
together" (namely, "because their combination is geometrically necessary") — a candidate that
had not been tried in rounds 114–117, and that is now closed with a clean null, the same shape
as those four attempts. **C41–C57 is honestly a fifth null attempt on OB1's broader question,
via a mechanism (NCG spectral-triple necessity) none of rounds 114–117 tried — and it failed
the same way they did.**
