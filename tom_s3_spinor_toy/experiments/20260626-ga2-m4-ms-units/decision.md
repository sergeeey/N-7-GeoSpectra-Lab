## Interpretation

**M_s is near-GUT scale** (~10^17 GeV), not TeV scale.
Radii ρ₆ ≈ 1.18 ℓ_s — compactification at string scale, not large extra dimensions.

**Cosmological moduli problem: NOT present.**
- Coughlan bound: m_mod > 30 TeV
- Our m_mod ≈ 3.79 × 10^11 TeV — 10^10 times above the bound
- Modulus decays rapidly, no overclosure issue

**For TeV-scale KK modes** (collider-accessible):
- Need M_s ≈ 927 GeV → ρ₆ ≈ 2.5 × 10^-19 m (sub-nuclear, 0.00025 fm)
- This is the opposite of ADD (which needs sub-mm dimensions)
- Our scenario is NOT a large-extra-dimension model

## What this does NOT mean
1. Does NOT claim M_s = 10^17 GeV is predicted — only that IF ρ₆ ≈ 1.18 ℓ_s THEN this scale follows
2. Does NOT validate the path constraint ρ₃ = ρ₆² in physical units
3. Does NOT map to the Standard Model gauge coupling scale without λ fixed (Bottleneck 1)
