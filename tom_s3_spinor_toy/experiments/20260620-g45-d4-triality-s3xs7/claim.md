---

## L0: Question Type

**Descriptive** — Does D₄ triality act non-trivially on S³×S⁷ spinors,  
and does this produce N_gen=3 within a single geometric compactification?

---

## Estimand

- **Population:** Fermionic excitations on S³×S⁷ (dim=10), one parallelization chosen
- **Intervention:** Triality τ: SO(8)→SO(8) acting on S⁷'s three parallelizations
- **Comparator:** Same geometry, different parallelization choice
- **Endpoint 1:** Is the triality orbit {P_v, P_s, P_c} trivial or non-trivial? (fixes G44 defect)
- **Endpoint 2:** Does one compactification on S³×S⁷ give 3 generations, or only 1?
- **MCID:** 1 vs 3 generations within a SINGLE geometric choice

---

## Falsifiable Claims

**H_G45a (triality visible):**  
S⁷ has full SO(8) symmetry, and the triality orbit of parallelizations has size 3 (not 1).  
→ Pre-registered: PASS (G44 revival condition was specifically this)

**H_G45b (N_gen=3 from geometry):**  
A single geometric compactification S³×S⁷ with one SO(8) structure gives 3 generations.  
→ Pre-registered: NULL

**H_G45c (all three together):**  
Taking the direct sum ψ_v ⊕ ψ_s ⊕ ψ_c of all three triality sectors in one theory gives SM-compatible N_gen=3.  
→ Pre-registered: WEAK — gives 3 copies but spinor count wrong for SM

> "In what world does H_G45b hold?"

- A world where the geometric compactification automatically includes all three parallelizations simultaneously
- OR: a world where the ALGEBRAIC sum ℍ⊗𝕆 is a valid geometric compactification

## What This Does NOT Mean

1. Does NOT kill the Furey-Hughes algebraic program (they work in ℂ⊗ℍ⊗𝕆, not S³×S⁷)
2. Does NOT mean triality is irrelevant — it IS visible on S⁷ (unlike S⁶)
3. Does NOT mean S³×S⁷ is wrong — it may be right for OTHER reasons (string dimension)
4. Does NOT prove N_gen=3 is impossible from octonionic geometry — only from single S³×S⁷

## Key Distinction (the whole point of G45)

| | G44 S³×S⁶ | G45 S³×S⁷ |
|--|-----------|-----------|
| Triality orbit size | 1 (collapses) | 3 (distinct) |
| N_gen per parallelization | 1 | 1 |
| N_gen if all 3 together | 1 | 3 (but non-geometric) |
| SM spinor count | 32=1 SM gen | 16 MW ≠ 32 |

The upgrade S⁶→S⁷ fixes the triality visibility problem but reveals a new problem:  
getting all three sectors into one theory is algebraic (Furey-Hughes), not geometric.
