# Claim — Round103 (D4 from goal-expansion-100): Is the Product-
Decoupling Ansatz Structurally Incompatible With `t=0`/`t=1` Coexistence?

**Question type:** Descriptive/conceptual (a logical-structure question
about what the ansatz does and does not forbid — not a new computation).

## Section 1 — Background

Goal-expansion-100's D4 asked: is `D_full²=D_{S3,t}²⊗I+I⊗D_{S6,twisted}²`
(E2/E12's decoupling assumption, presupposed throughout E1-E101)
STRUCTURALLY INCOMPATIBLE with any mechanism forcing `t=0` and `t=1` to
coexist — the highest-impact, highest-risk "moonshot" item, since a
confirmed incompatibility would require abandoning the ansatz entirely to
make further progress on this research line.

## Section 2 — Pre-registered criteria

- **INCOMPATIBLE (moonshot confirmed):** a rigorous argument shows the
  ansatz, as literally stated (a single connection ∇^t on a single S³
  factor), cannot support ANY construction where both `t=0`- and
  `t=1`-sourced zero modes appear as separate massless 4D fields in the
  same low-energy spectrum.
- **COMPATIBLE, NO STRUCTURAL OBSTRUCTION:** coexistence (as SEPARATE
  postulated fermion multiplets, one built from `D_{S3,0}`, one from
  `D_{S3,1}`, both entering the total Lagrangian as a direct sum) is
  shown to be a perfectly consistent field-theoretic construction WITHIN
  the ansatz — meaning the real, still-open gap is not "coexistence is
  forbidden" but "no mechanism has been found that REQUIRES it" (already
  the finding of rounds 90-101).
- **REFRAMING NEEDED:** the original D4 question itself is shown to
  conflate two logically distinct questions ("is coexistence ALLOWED" vs.
  "is coexistence REQUIRED") — report this honestly rather than forcing
  an answer to the question as originally, possibly imprecisely, posed.
