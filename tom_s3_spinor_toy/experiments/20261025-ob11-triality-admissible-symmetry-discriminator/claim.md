# Claim — OB11 discriminator: are the triality channels separated by admissible (G-invariant) projectors?

**Pre-registered:** 2026-10-25, before running `discriminator_ob11.py`.
**Status:** RUN — structural result on the internal index only; NOT a zero-mode or generation count.
**Question type:** Descriptive (L0). It describes the symmetry structure of an existing construction. No causal claim.
**Relates to:** OPEN_BLOCKERS OB11 (matter-generation factorisation; WEAK reading unresolved). NOT OB13 — OB13 is the t = 0 vs t = 1 selector.

## Estimand

- **Population:** the internal 8-dim index of the Round59 construction (`round59_route_a_independent.py`, `spin_lift(ADNU[a])`).
- **Admissible group G:** su(3) generated by X_1..X_8, which commute with the Round59 Dirac matrix D under the Leibniz lift L(X) (C78: commutant of D equals su(3), dim 8).
- **Endpoint 1:** dimension of the commutant of {X_a} in End(C^8).
- **Endpoint 2:** the rank of the smallest G-invariant projector that contains a triality block.
- **Endpoint 3:** whether a rank-1 projector inside a non-singlet multiplet is G-invariant (negative control; must be False).

## Decision rule (fixed before the run)

- commutant dim 1 → 8 is one irreducible block; no invariant split.
- commutant dim 6 with blocks 3 + 3bar + 1 + 1 → triality is an irreducible triplet block under G; the three channels are rotated into each other by G; they are not independent G-invariant sectors. Minimal invariant projector on the triplet has rank 3.
- commutant dim 2 → two-block split (not expected).

## Kill criterion

If a G-invariant rank-1 projector onto a single triality channel exists AND commutes with D, the three channels are separable under the admissible group, and the multiplicity-3 is not removable by G. This would argue for independent sectors (OB11 WEAK reading strengthened).

Conversely, the result above (pattern 3 + 3bar + 1 + 1, commutant 6) supports the reading that the triplet is one G-multiplet — the factor 3 is removable by G unless the physical action breaks su(3).

## Result (run 2026-10-25)

- Casimir on C^8: eigenvalue 4/3 with multiplicity 6, eigenvalue 0 with multiplicity 2 → 3 + 3bar + 1 + 1.
- Commutant of {X_a} in End(C^8): dimension 6.
- Negative control: a rank-1 projector inside the non-singlet 6-dim block is not G-invariant (PASS).
- Verdict printed: `TRIALITY_IS_INVARIANT_BLOCK__NO_RANK1_INVARIANT_SECTOR`.

## What this does NOT mean

1. It is NOT a count of physical zero modes. The 64x64 matrix D is the algebraic fibre matrix: its kernel is 36-dimensional, which is a property of the algebraic matrix, not of the differential operator. Zero modes of the differential operator are discussed in round59 (rank 1 per channel on the SU(3)-invariant domain).
2. It does NOT show that the zero modes of the differential operator decompose as 3 + 3bar + 1 + 1. That step is NOT done here (next step below).
3. It does NOT establish that the physical action preserves su(3). If the physical action breaks G, the triplet can split and this test does not apply.
4. It does NOT address exchange statistics or the t = 0 vs t = 1 selector (OB13).
