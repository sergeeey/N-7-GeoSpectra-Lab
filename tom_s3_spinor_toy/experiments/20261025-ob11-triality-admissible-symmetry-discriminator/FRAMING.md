# Two programmes for the N-7 GeoSpectra Lab (framing, not a result)

**Construction-first:** the twist S⁻, the finite algebra, the representation choices, and the parameters are allowed as explicit inputs. The test is internal mathematical consistency (spectrum, index, commutants, intertwiners). This is what the project does today in most rounds.

**Principle-first:** the same objects must follow from an independently stated action, symmetry, topology and reduction rule. Inputs that do not follow are kept free and are NOT counted as predictions.

**Why it matters:** comparing the two shows which part of the programme is reconstruction (fixed by inputs), which part is a mathematical result inside the model, and which part could carry a physical prediction. The parent-action question (OB1, parked) and the triality-independence question (OB11, this experiment's subject) are the places where the two programmes diverge.

**Status:** framing only. Principle-first has not been run; its first required artefact is a stated 13-dimensional action, which OB1 says has not been found.
