# Claim — S3 spin connection to Tom operators

## Claim

The Hopf-frame S spin connection contains coordinate components matching the
angular coefficient structure seen in Tom's S spinor operators.

## Scope

This is a local differential-geometric check. It compares the unit S Hopf
coframe, the torsion-free Cartan equations, and the resulting spin connection
components.

## Pass condition

The Cartan structure equations must be satisfied by the stated coframe and
connection one-forms.

## Falsifier

If the listed connection one-forms fail the Cartan check, the local geometric
interpretation is not supported.
