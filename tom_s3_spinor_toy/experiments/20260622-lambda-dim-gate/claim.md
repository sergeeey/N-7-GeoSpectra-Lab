λ = c · ρ₆² (up to fixed rational factors of κ = ρ₃/ρ₆) on the
compactification trajectory, making exp(−λ/ρ₆²) a numerical constant

**FAIL criterion:**  A geometric mechanism produces λ with a NEW length scale
                     Λ ≠ ρ₃, ρ₆ — but then it is NOT a pure geometric mechanism.
