`print_only_scan.py` (heuristic, many false positives): 19 of 21 scripts of C222-C228 contain no `assert` and no `sys.exit`, so they exit 0 even when a check prints FAIL. `tom_line_check.py` mitigates this by textual failure detection (a `FAIL ` line, a non-empty `FAILS:` line, `Traceback`) on the logs of pinned runs; a bare run of those scripts does not. Prints without a test remain in `independent_subagent_check_s3_lift.py` (C228; Phi, Gram printed) and in C226 line 179 (the per-point V values printed).

## What this does NOT imply

- that the unmodified scripts are wrong: the survivors are equivalent mutants and the C222-C228 numbers were re-run (C225, C226 exit 0, no FAIL)
- completeness: operators and sample are small; semantic mutations such as erasing a convention (found by the skeptic review) are not generated by these operators

## Caveat

Three of the mutants that crash change `range(...)` bounds and indices; a crash proves nothing about the checks.
