1. psi = xi / r obeys D_a psi = -1/2 sigma_a psi for a = 1, 2, 3, with ladder values D_+ psi_1 = -psi_2, D_- psi_2 = -psi_1, D_- psi_1 = D_+ psi_2 = 0.
