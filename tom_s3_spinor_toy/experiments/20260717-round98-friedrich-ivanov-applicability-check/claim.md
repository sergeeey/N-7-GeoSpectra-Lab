# Claim — Round98 (C5 from goal-expansion-100): Friedrich-Ivanov "At Most
One Torsion Connection" Applicability to S³=SU(2)

**Question type:** Descriptive (literature-applicability check).

## Section 1 — Background

Friedrich & Ivanov (2002), "Parallel spinors and connections with
skew-symmetric torsion in string theory," *Asian J. Math.* 6, 303-335,
arXiv:math/0102142, prove that for almost-contact-metric, almost-Hermitian,
and G₂-structures, **at most one** connection with totally skew-symmetric
torsion exists. If this uniqueness theorem applies to S³=SU(2) with its
round bi-invariant metric, it would be a structural NO-GO for the entire
premise of "why do t=0 AND t=1 physically coexist" — because the
Cartan-Schouten family {∇^t} would then not be two equally-canonical
alternative torsion-connections on one structure, but a family in which at
most one member can be "the" torsion connection singled out by the
structure itself.

## Section 2 — Pre-registered criteria

- **NO-GO CONFIRMED:** the paper's uniqueness theorem is shown (via its
  actual hypotheses, read directly) to apply to 3-dimensional almost-
  contact-metric structures / to S³=SU(2) specifically.
- **NO-GO DOES NOT APPLY:** the theorem's hypotheses are shown to exclude
  n=3 or the specific bi-invariant-metric-on-a-group case.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** available sources (WebFetch of arXiv abstract page,
  PDF, and a related survey) do not resolve the applicability question one
  way or the other — report as `<unknown>`, not as a resolved no-go.
