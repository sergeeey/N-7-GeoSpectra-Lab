S⁷ has exactly three coset space descriptions related by SO(8) triality:
{SO(8)/SO(7), Sp(2)/Sp(1), Spin(7)/G₂} = the three parallelizations.  
→ Pre-registered: PASS (known mathematical fact)

**H_G46b (simultaneous geometric presence):**  
A single metric on S⁷ can carry all three coset structures simultaneously as metric-compatible structures.  
→ Pre-registered: NULL — choosing a metric = choosing one parallelization

**H_G46c (3-Sasakian fixes this):**  
The 3-Sasakian structure of Sp(2)/Sp(1) = S⁷ spans all three triality sectors.  
→ Pre-registered: NULL — 3-Sasakian gives THREE Sasakian structures WITHIN ONE triality sector,
not spanning across all three sectors

**H_G46d (parent space SO(8)):**  
SO(8) simultaneously contains all three representations, providing a geometric realization.  
→ Pre-registered: PASS on existence, NULL on utility (dim(SO(8))=28, not a compactification)

## Counterfactual Frame

> "In what world does a geometric realization of all three triality sectors simultaneously exist?"

A world where there is a compact manifold M such that:
1. M carries a natural SO(8) action
2. The three isotropy subgroups SO(7), SO(7)', SO(7)'' are all simultaneously defined on M
3. M has dimension ≤ 10 (string theory constraint) or ≤ 9 (our S³×S⁶ framework)

Count of independent changes needed:
1. Find a new coset space (not S⁷) that has SO(8) symmetry but smaller dimension than SO(8) itself
2. Show this space carries all three reps simultaneously
3. Show it admits a spinor structure compatible with SM fermions

≥3 changes from known geometry → claim is extremely speculative → pre-register as NULL.
