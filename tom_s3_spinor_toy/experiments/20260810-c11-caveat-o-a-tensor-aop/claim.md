## What was left open, and why it matters

C52 proved orientability fails because `π_D(c)` is **local** while `γ = U_ι⊗s1` is not.
That used the `A`-valued Hochschild cycle. Several standard statements of the axiom use a
cycle in `Z_n(A, A⊗A°)`, so `π_D` carries **`J b* J⁻¹` factors too**:

```
pi_D(c) = SUM  a0 . (J b0* J^-1) . [D,a1] ... [D,an]
```

Those extra factors are local **provided `JAJ⁻¹` is local** — which C50/C51 established
only when `A` contains the **full** twisted diagonal. C52's own regime (`B` smaller) is
exactly where that was **not** established. **So the escape, if there is one, is: `J`
supplies the `U_ι` that the algebra cannot.**

That is not a formality. Shrinking `B` genuinely enlarges `A'`:
- with `B =` the **`ι`-even** functions, `A'`'s entries acquire a `U_ι`-carrying part —
  `m·U_ι` commutes with `b` exactly when `b∘ι = b`;
- with `B = C·1`, `A'` is everything.

## The claim under test

> **C53 (proposed).** In the `A⊗A°` formulation there is an admissible `(A, J)` with a
> smaller diagonal part for which `J b* J⁻¹` carries `U_ι`, making `γ` reachable as
> `π_D(c)` and repairing orientability.

**Falsifier, fixed in advance:** if every `U_ι`-carrying operator has an **unbounded**
commutator with `D`, then `J b* J⁻¹` — whose commutator is bounded because `[D,b*]` is —
cannot carry `U_ι`, and C53 is **REFUTED**.

## The honest residual I expect to remain

`J u J⁻¹` could in principle be a **sum** in which a `U_ι` term's unbounded commutator
**cancels** against another unbounded term. The finite model cannot exclude that, because
it has no unbounded operators. I will name it **CAVEAT O′** rather than let the verdict
imply more than the computation supports.

## What this cannot show

  (e.g. `sign(D_M)⊗s1`) with bounded `[D,·]` — those are non-local but carry no `U_ι`,
  so O3's `H_M`-factor argument, not locality, is what closes that case.

- Nothing about `N_gen = 3` — step 7 untouched by agreement.
