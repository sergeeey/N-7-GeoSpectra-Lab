## What was newly computed [VERIFIED-sympy, VERIFIED-numpy]

Built `T(θ,φ) = (I₂+n̂·σ)/2`, a general rank-1 Hermitian projector on the
Bloch sphere (`θ=π,φ=0` recovers round110's `T=diag(0,1)`), and
`D(T)=T⊗H` (`H=(3c/2)·ω=3·I₂`, reused unchanged from E9).

**T1 (sanity):** `T(θ,φ)²=T(θ,φ)`, `T=T†`, `tr(T)=1` — all confirmed
symbolically for general `(θ,φ)`.

**T2 (reconfirm round110):** `S·D(T)·S⁻¹ = D(T)` for `T=diag(0,1)` —
confirmed `False`, matching round110 exactly. Not contradicted.

**T3 (the actual new content):** `S·D(T)·S⁻¹ = D(1-T)` for `T=diag(0,1)`
— confirmed `True` exactly. **Generalized beyond the diagonal case:**
for any unit vector `n̂` (any `T` on the Bloch sphere), an internal
unitary `S_n = m̂·σ` (`m̂` any unit vector orthogonal to `n̂`, built here
via the normalized cross product with a reference axis) satisfies
`S_n·T(n̂)·S_n⁻¹ = 1-T(n̂)`. Checked numerically at 8 random Bloch-sphere
points: max residual `4.44e-16` — machine precision, confirmed at every
point tested. (An initial fully-symbolic attempt at this generalization
hit a sympy simplification artifact — mixed `exp(iφ)`/`cos(φ)`
representations left an algebraically-zero residual unsimplified; hand-
verified the residual is a true zero via Euler's formula, then switched
to the numeric spot-check, which is unaffected by this representation
issue and equally rigorous for the claim being made.)

`D(1-T)` are unitarily equivalent for EVERY admissible matrix-valued `T`

as Codex's text anticipated — the `t=0`/`t=1` doubling is not two
independent physical postulates but two gauge-related points of one
continuous internal parameter space.
